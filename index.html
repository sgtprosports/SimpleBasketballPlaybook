Man-to-Man: Pressure Deny

Defense
Full Pressure Setup
Deny First Pass & Hip Alignment

Core Objective & Strategy

An aggressive baseline defensive scheme designed to suffocate perimeter ball handlers and deny easy passing lanes across the entire half-court.

Execution Mechanics

  • Ball Pressure: On-ball defender dictates direction by sitting on the hip of the primary ball handler, forcing them away from the middle toward the sideline trap zones.
  • Perimeter Denial: Adjacent wing defenders play in 'full denial' stance with one hand in the passing lane, forcing high arc lob passes if the offense attempts a skip.
  • Help & Recover: Weak-side defenders drop one step into the paint to form an invisible wall, ready to stunt or take a charge on any interior driving attempts.

Key Coaching Read

Keep active feet and communicate ball screens early before the screener establishes contact.
