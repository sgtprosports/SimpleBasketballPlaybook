Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

PlummerHouse Enterprises LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Master Basketball Tactical Playbook mobile application and progressive web application (PWA). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our app functions as an offline-capable tactical playbook and matchup assistant for basketball coaches. We do not require user registration, login accounts, or personal profile creation to access tactical sets. As a result, we do not actively collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number through the core application.

2. Data Storage and Local Device Caching

The application may utilize local device storage (such as browser local storage or PWA cache mechanisms) solely to store user preferences, active playbook settings, and offline assets to ensure reliable performance. This data remains securely on your device and is not transmitted to external servers by us.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Our application is distributed via digital app stores (such as Google Play). Third-party platform providers may collect standard diagnostic data, crash logs, and installation telemetry in accordance with their respective privacy policies to ensure platform security and app stability.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed as a coaching tool for sports professionals and youth basketball coaches. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
